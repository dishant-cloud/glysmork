# Beta Tester Outreach Templates

Here are several templates tailored for different platforms and target audiences. **Always personalize the first sentence** based on their recent posts or bio to prove you aren't a bot.

---

## 1. Instagram DM (The "Deep Connection" Angle)
*Best for people who post about psychology, philosophy, mental health, or frustration with modern dating/networking apps.*

**Subject/Opener:** Hey [Name], loved your recent post about [Topic]!

**Message:**
> Hey [Name], 
> 
> I loved your recent post/story about [insert topic—e.g., how superficial dating apps are / the importance of real conversations]. I completely agree, and it’s actually why my team just built **Glysmork**.
> 
> It’s an AI-driven matchmaking engine designed exclusively for deep connections. Instead of swiping on selfies, you take a psychological quiz, our AI generates an abstract visual representation of your "soul", and then you search for people based purely on mutual intent (whether for dating, mentorship, or just deep chats).
> 
> We are in a closed Beta right now and are looking for 100 thoughtful people to test the AI engine. Since you clearly value authentic connections, I’d love for you to try it out and tell me if the AI reads you accurately. 
> 
> Can I send you a VIP access link?

---

## 2. Reddit DM (The "Tech/AI Innovator" Angle)
*Best for subreddits like r/SideProject, r/ArtificialInteligence, or r/dating.*

**Subject:** An AI that matches people by psychological intent, not selfies (Beta Test)

**Message:**
> Hey u/[Username], 
> 
> I saw your comments on the [Subreddit Name] thread and thought you might appreciate the tech we are building. 
> 
> We just launched the beta for **Glysmork**. It’s a universal connection engine. Instead of standard profile bios, users have a live conversation with an AI, which generates a deep psychological profile and vectorizes their intent. You can then search our database using natural language (e.g., "Find me a founder in NY who loves stoicism").
> 
> We also have a live AI monitor running in the chat to detect manipulative behavior or bots.
> 
> We need our first 100 beta testers to stress-test the LLM matchmaking and the WebRTC video calls. Let me know if you'd be down to break the system and give us some brutal feedback!

## 3. LinkedIn DM (The "Networking/Mentorship" Angle)
*Best for professionals, founders, or people looking for mentors.*

**Subject:** Beta testing a new AI networking engine

**Message:**
> Hi [Name],
> 
> I’ve been following your journey at [Company] and admire your approach to [Skill/Industry].
> 
> I’m the founder of **Glysmork**, a new AI-powered platform designed to fix the noise in modern networking. Our engine uses LLMs to map your core traits and expertise, allowing you to instantly connect with mentors, founders, or collaborators based on highly specific intent queries, bypassing the usual cold outreach.
> 
> We are rolling out our private Beta this week. Given your background, your feedback on the accuracy of the AI matching would be incredibly valuable. 
> 
> Would you be open to trying it out for 5 minutes? I'd love to comp you a premium account in exchange for your thoughts.
> 
> Best,
> [Your Name]

---

## Tips for High Conversion:
1. **Don't send links in the first message:** Social media algorithms often flag DMs with links from strangers as spam. Ask a question first ("Can I send you a link?"). Once they reply "Yes", send the URL.
2. **Offer value:** Telling them they get a "Premium account" for life in exchange for testing is a great hook.
3. **Keep it short:** If the templates above feel too long for your personal style, cut them in half. Focus on the core hook: *AI Matchmaking based on psychology, not selfies.*

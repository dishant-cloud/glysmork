# Instagram Launch Content Strategy

Here is exactly what you should post to transition your personal audience to the brand page, and how to populate the brand page itself.

---

## 1. From Your Personal Account (Stories)
*Goal: Drive your current friends/followers to follow the new page without being overly "salesy". Use native Instagram text features.*

**Story 1: The "Curiosity" Hook (Text on a dark aesthetic background)**
> "I've been working on something crazy behind the scenes for the past 2 months..."

**Story 2: The Problem (Text + Poll sticker)**
> "Honestly, modern dating/networking apps feel broken. It's just endless swiping on selfies with zero actual connection. 
> 
> [POLL STICKER: "Agree" / "Disagree"]"

**Story 3: The Reveal (Screen recording of the Glysmork AI chat/UI)**
> "So my team and I built **Glysmork**. 
> An AI-driven matchmaking engine that matches you based on your *psychology and intent*, not just your face. The AI literally generates an abstract 'soul' image of you based on how you talk."

**Story 4: The Call to Action (Text + "@glysmork" tag)**
> "We are doing a closed Beta test for 100 people this week.
> Drop a follow on @[YourGlysmorkInstaHandle] — I'll be DMing VIP access links to early followers!"

---

## 2. Glysmork Brand Account (Stories)
*Goal: Post these to the Glysmork account so that when people land on the profile from your personal page, they see active, high-value content.*

**Brand Story 1: The Manifesto**
> "No more swiping. No more shallow bios. 
> Welcome to Glysmork: The world's first intent-based psychological connection engine."

**Brand Story 2: Feature Highlight (Screenshot of the AI Lie Detector/Chat analysis)**
> "The engine runs a live AI monitor in the background. If someone is being manipulative, overly creepy, or acting like a bot, the AI instantly flags them and generates a visual warning."

**Brand Story 3: How it Works (Carousel of 3 fast clips)**
> 1. Chat with our AI for 2 minutes to generate your psychological profile.
> 2. Search for exact connections (e.g., "Find me a founder into philosophy").
> 3. Connect via secure, real-time WebRTC video/audio.
